Your Privacy Is Important To Us

This Privacy Policy explains how Selena Massage Spa collects, uses, stores and handles personal information when you visit our website, submit a booking request or contact us.

By using our website and submitting information through our forms, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required, we will request it separately.

Last Updated: 25 November 2026

01. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our website and services.

Booking Information

When you submit an appointment request, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your country
  • Your selected service or treatment
  • Your preferred appointment date and time
  • Special requests or additional messages
  • Your selected payment method
  • A transaction reference, if provided
  • An optional payment screenshot, if uploaded

Contact Form Information

When you contact us through our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if provided
  • Your enquiry subject
  • The message you send us

Technical Information

When you visit our website, our website hosting provider or other technical services may process limited technical information, such as browser details, device information, IP address and server activity logs, depending on their configurations.

02. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our website may be used for the following purposes:

  • To receive and review appointment requests.
  • To communicate with customers about their bookings.
  • To respond to contact enquiries and questions.
  • To identify the service or treatment requested.
  • To review payment references and supporting payment information.
  • To manage appointment-related communications and records.
  • To help maintain the security and proper operation of our website.
  • To meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We aim to use personal information only for purposes that are relevant to the reason it was collected or another lawful purpose.

03. Booking Forms and Contact Forms

Our website uses online forms to receive booking requests and customer enquiries. These forms currently use Formspree as a form submission service.

Information submitted through these forms is transmitted to Formspree for processing and may be stored in the associated Formspree account.

Formspree may also process information according to its own privacy policy and service terms.

You can review its privacy information here: Formspree Privacy Policy .

Our booking and contact forms currently use the same Formspree project. This means submissions from both forms may appear in the same Formspree account.

04. Payment Information

Our booking form may ask you to select a payment method and provide a transaction reference or optional payment screenshot.

This information may be used to help review and verify a payment associated with an appointment request.

Payment Safety

Never submit the following information through our website forms:

  • Bank card PINs
  • Card security codes (CVV)
  • Full card passwords or banking passwords
  • Cryptocurrency wallet private keys
  • Wallet recovery phrases or seed phrases

Our website forms are not designed to collect or store your full card details or cryptocurrency wallet credentials.

If a hosted card payment service is introduced, its payment processing and privacy terms will also apply.

05. How We Share Information

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information.

Information may be made available to service providers where necessary to operate our website and handle customer requests. These may include:

  • Formspree, for online form submissions.
  • Website hosting providers, for website operation and security.
  • Payment service providers, if introduced and used for a payment.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.
